# QA — s16-validate-chain

[Story](../../stories.md) · [Plan](../../plans/s16-validate-chain.md)

Scope: the story's surface, what `git diff main...story/s16-validate-chain` touches on it, and the acceptance criteria its user can observe through it; nothing else was checked.
Driver: scenarios.md of qa-drivers — surface endpoint, platform lib (setting `Platform: lib, cli`; the `cli` row applies only to a `command` surface)
Evidence: transcripts
Tool: Bash. The walk ran in a `mktemp -d` copy of `git ls-files`, where `cargo fetch` and `cargo build --workspace --all-features` both exited 0. It used `qa-scen`, a scratch binary crate quoted whole at the end of each transcript, rebuilt unchanged from the source the previous walk quoted. `qa-scen` depends by path on `nacre-validate`, `nacre-pades`, `nacre-pdf` and `nacre-core`, and calls the public `validate`, `validate_with_anchors`, `TrustAnchors` and `build_chain`. The s02 bench PKI was generated afresh in the copy with `conformance/pki/generate.sh` (OpenSSL 3.6.5). The bench PDFs were signed with `nacre_pades::sign` (`qa-scen bench-sign`). Extra certificates were forged with OpenSSL, and the scripts are quoted in the transcripts. `/DSS` revisions were appended with `nacre_pdf`'s `Update` (`qa-scen dss`). No container, DSS or responder was started. This walk replaces the one made on tree `2982a229…`, before commit `7ece192` (EKU judged on the signer only, `id-kp-clientAuth` accepted). Every scenario of that walk was replayed, in two full passes that matched byte for byte. Its outcomes are unchanged except the three EKU cases that `7ece192` changes on purpose, and nine new EKU scenarios were added. `finding-1-decoy-budget.txt` and `finding-1-pool-limit.txt` are kept as the record of earlier trees.

- DSS's verdict on the same chains, including the EKU rule of `7ece192`: DSS was not started; the s18 campaign makes that comparison.
- RSA-signed certificate paths. Every certificate tried is ECDSA P-256, and the corpus and the bench hold no RSA chain.
- Name matching across different encodings of the same name (RFC 5280 §7.1). No such certificates were forged.
